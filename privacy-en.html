Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026 · Türkçe olarak oku

At EEGameStudio ("we", "us"), we care about the privacy of people who use the games and applications we develop ("Services"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Our games do not directly ask you for personal information (name, address, phone number, etc.). However, the following information may be collected automatically for the Services to function:

  • Device information (model, operating system version, language settings)
  • Anonymous device identifiers such as advertising IDs — known as IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) on iOS and GAID (Google Advertising ID) on Android
  • In-game usage and performance data
  • Crash reports and error logs

On iOS devices, your explicit consent is requested before IDFA is collected, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. On Android devices, GAID can be managed and reset from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

2. How We Use This Information

  • To ensure our games function properly and to improve them
  • To detect and fix errors
  • To display ads and measure ad performance
  • To analyze usage statistics

3. Third-Party Services

Our games may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies, for example:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

4. Children's Privacy

Our Services do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately. If you are aware of such a situation, please contact us.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect the limited information collected through the app. However, we remind you that no transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

ColorVolt does not itself store personal data, so there is no data on our end to delete. You can manage your advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID) preferences directly from your device settings. For questions about your rights under the data protection laws applicable to your country (for example KVKK in Turkey, GDPR in the European Union, or CCPA in California), please contact us.

7. Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes take effect as soon as they are published on this page.
